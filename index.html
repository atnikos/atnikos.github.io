Nikos Athanasiou

Research Scientist at Flexion Robotics, Zürich · 3D humans, motion and language

I work on understanding and generating human movement — the intricate details of how people move, and how language can be used to describe, generate and control 3D human motion.

Before that I was a Ph.D. student at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, in the Perceiving Systems department, where I was lucky to be supervised by Michael J. Black and Gül Varol.

Earlier, I did my B.S. & M.Eng. (Diploma) at ECE, NTUA in Athens. Back then I did research on natural language representations at the Speech and Language Processing group under the supervision of Alexandros Potamianos. I also worked for a while at Deeplab, during its early stages, with a wonderful team of people.

Research

My research aims to understand the intricate details of human movement and to generate fine-grained 3D human motions. I am interested in 3D humans and their interactions with other humans and with their environment.

I like working with video, and ideally I want to use every kind of data available to train big models. I am also really interested in exploiting large VLMs and LLMs in smart ways to synthesize data and build on top of them.
